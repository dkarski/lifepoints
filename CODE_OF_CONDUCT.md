# Code of conduct

Be respectful and keep discussions focused on the project. Constructive disagreement is welcome;
harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and sharing private information without consent are not.

These rules apply to this repository's issues, pull requests and other project spaces.
The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep them welcoming and useful.

Report conduct concerns privately to Daniel Karski at [daniel.karski5q@gmail.com](mailto:daniel.karski5q@gmail.com).
Reports will be handled confidentially as far as possible. For security vulnerabilities, follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
