## 1.0.0 (2026-11-20)

First release.

### New

* Life counter for two players, with starting life 20, 30, 40 or any custom value.
* The current game is saved and can be continued after closing the app.
* Light and dark themes.
* English and Polish, following the phone's language.
* Works fully offline and asks for no permissions.
* About, privacy policy, terms of use and open source licenses in the app.
* Contact with the developer and an optional tip jar (QR code).
