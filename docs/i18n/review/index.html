Layout fixes found through the screenshots

  • Coming soon screen now scrolls: in Portuguese and French the “Copy e-mail” button was pushed under the bottom bar after “Contact developer” found no e-mail app. Texts were shortened too, so these languages fit without scrolling.
  • Contact button wrapped to two lines inside the fixed-height button in Tamil, Catalan, Croatian and Georgian; shortened (found by capturing Home, Coming soon, Settings and Game menu in all 54 other languages with L10N_QUICK=1 scripts/l10n_shots.sh). Catalan and Galician “Custom” tiles now read Altre / Outro.

Open points

  • The keyboard line “Suggest contact names? Touch for info.” on the Custom life and search screens comes from the emulator's keyboard, not from the app. On the Kompakt the MuditaOS keyboard is shown.
  • All translations were generated with AI. The 9 languages here were reviewed in UI context; a native speaker review is still recommended before calling them final. The other 54 languages were checked only technically (scripts/i18n_check.py: complete, placeholders, escaping).
  • Urdu renders in Naskh instead of the expected Nastaliq (no Nastaliq font on MuditaOS).
  • The legal texts (Privacy, Terms of use, Legal notes) are translations for convenience; the English text is the reference.
