# Lifepoints - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Lifepoints is a life counter app for Mudita Kompakt, made by Daniel Karski (contact: daniel.karski5q@gmail.com).

## Summary
Lifepoints does not collect, send or share any personal data.

## What the app stores
The current game (starting life, players, life totals) and your theme choice are stored only on your phone.
They are deleted when you uninstall the app. They are not included in phone backups.

## Network and permissions
The app works offline. It has no internet permission, asks for no permissions and never connects to anything.
It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Links that leave the app
- Contact: opens your e-mail app with the developer's address, or copies the address. Nothing is sent by the app.
- Tip jar: shows a QR code with a Ko-fi link to scan with another device. The app itself opens nothing. Tips are
  handled entirely by Ko-fi under its own privacy policy and are voluntary; they unlock nothing in the app.

## Children
The app does not collect personal data from children or any other users.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on the app's InkApps page
with a new effective date.
